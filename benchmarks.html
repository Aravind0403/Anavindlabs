Measurement Methodology & Hardware Baselines

Benchmarks & Provenance Protocol

The defining differentiator of this lab is that every number carries its own provenance. When I publish a latency, throughput, or cost figure, it is accompanied by the exact hardware tier, dataset source, and an unambiguous declaration of whether it was measured on physical silicon or projected analytically.

Operating Discipline

The Three Provenance Rules

Rule 1 Never Merge Hardware Baselines

Numbers from high-TFLOPS consumer cards (RTX 4090) and cloud virtualization instances (GCP L4) are never aggregated or blended into composite averages. They are presented in dedicated columns with their respective driver and PCIe setups.

Rule 2 In-Sentence Provenance Framing

If a metric was generated through trace replay or discrete-event simulation (e.g. Alibaba cluster logs), it must be labeled as "modeled" or "simulated" directly in the sentence that reports it, never relegated to fine-print footnotes.

Rule 3 Disconfirmed Results as Primary Output

An engineering trade-off where an optimization kernel runs slower or where an architectural assumption breaks down is published with the same prominence as positive gains.

Lab Notes & Empirical Insights

Key Architectural Disclosures

Disconfirmed Result ShareGPT vs Alpaca

Synthetic Instruction Skew in Length Predictors

When training the Clairvoyant output-length classifier, instruction-tuning datasets (e.g. Stanford Alpaca) created model collapse. Models trained on synthetic prompts achieved near-zero ranking accuracy on real conversational traces. Synthetic instruction pairs are constrained by GPT distillation brevity, whereas real human conversations exhibit multi-modal bimodal length distributions with high variance.

Ablation Finding Alibaba 2018 Cluster Trace

Stateless EMA vs. Deep LSTM in Job Routing

In a predictor ablation for GPU job routing, evaluating 14 predictor variants revealed that an Exponential Moving Average (EMA) with decay factor α=0.2 routed 89.2% of jobs to stable nodes, beating the LSTM's 75.4%. The LSTM suffered from catastrophic forgetting when burst patterns shifted, while the stateless EMA adapted in one step with zero inference latency.

Incident Analysis vLLM Memory Thrashing

TrainSight: Resolving the 93% Throughput Drop

An aggressive VRAM allocation target under ungated input sequence variance caused severe block fragmentation in PagedAttention, triggering sudden 81%–93% throughput collapses. Rather than backing off VRAM limits, TrainSight’s sub-millisecond CPU length-variance gate bin-packs requests, boosting batch throughput by 81.0% and reducing padding waste by 40.2%.

Kernel Profiling FP8 Quantization Overhead

Kernel Overhead vs. Network Amortization

In HeteroDisagg, transforming FP16 KV-cache blocks into FP8 introduces +11ms to +32ms of isolated compute delay. On high-bandwidth fabric (NVLink), this is an unviable trade-off. On PCIe x16 or commodity 10GbE interconnects between disaggregated prefill and decode nodes, the 2.0x byte reduction overcomes the kernel overhead, resulting in a net latency win.
