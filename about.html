Background & Practice Scope

Systems Engineering & LLM Infrastructure

With an engineering background rooted in large-scale distributed systems at Microsoft (Hyderabad) and Amazon (Chennai), I founded Anavind Labs to investigate, benchmark, and harden the core LLM serving and GPU infrastructure stack.

Having designed systems ranging from multi-service enterprise governance platforms to real-time hardware test schedulers, I bring the same operational rigor to AI runtimes: declare every hardware baseline, distinguish empirical telemetry from analytical models, and treat negative results as primary systems insights.

Operating Principles

Research Manifesto

1. Precision Over Hype

No marketing superlatives. Every performance claim explicitly details the model checkpoint, concurrency profile, batching policy, input/output sequence distributions, and exact GPU topology.

2. Explicit Separation of Fact from Model

Measurements derived from discrete-event simulations, analytical projections, or synthetic trace replays are clearly designated as modeled data. Live hardware telemetry stands on its own.

3. Disconfirmed Results Carry Equal Value

Negative results are primary systems insights. When an intricate scheduler underperforms a simple FIFO queue, or an experimental kernel adds more launch overhead than wire savings, the disconfirmed result is published openly with complete profiling traces.
